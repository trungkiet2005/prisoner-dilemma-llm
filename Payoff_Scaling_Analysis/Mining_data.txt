# 4. CORE RESEARCH QUESTION

The central scientific question is:

> Does changing the numerical magnitude of payoffs alter LLM behavior even though the strategic ordering of the prisoner's dilemma remains unchanged?

Treat λ as the primary experimental variable.

Do NOT simply compare raw payoff.

Separate:

### Mechanical payoff scaling

Raw penalties necessarily change with λ.

from:

### Behavioral scaling

The following should NOT mechanically change simply because λ changes:

* cooperation probability
* strategy choice
* outcome frequencies
* defection behavior
* cooperation persistence
* response to opponent actions
* endgame behavior
* strategy composition

This distinction is critical.
