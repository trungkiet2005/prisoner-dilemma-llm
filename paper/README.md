## Known issues carried into the text

Both are stated in the manuscript rather than hidden, because a reviewer
would find them:

- **Horizon confound.** Only the Gemini arm was told the round count. Marked
  as a confound in Figure 1b and argued in §3.5 to work *against* the
  conclusion drawn from it.
- **Objective-framing confound.** The FAIRGAME templates ask English, French
  and Vietnamese agents to *minimise a penalty* but Arabic and Chinese agents
  to *maximise a reward*. The language analysis is therefore re-run within the
  three languages that share one framing (§3.3). This costs one of the three
  originally significant language effects (Gemini) and is reported as such.
